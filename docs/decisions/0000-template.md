# NNNN. Short title of the decision

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN | Rejected
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What situation requires a decision? Describe the problem, the constraints and
the facts that matter. Do not describe the solution here.

## Decision

What was decided. One or two short paragraphs, in the active voice
("We store raw files as Parquet under `data/raw/`").

## Alternatives considered

- **Alternative A** - what it is, and why it was not chosen.
- **Alternative B** - what it is, and why it was not chosen.

## Consequences

What changes because of this decision: what becomes easier, what becomes
harder, what is now ruled out, and any follow-up work it creates.
